Reading the chart. Each market row shows the campaign cadence per channel. Circles are discrete moments (a push send, an email send, a broadcast). Bars are continuous (in-app banner, Promocard, help centre) — segmented to show how the content pack changes between phases and movements. Diamonds mark cohort waves in Movement 1 (10% / +15% / +25% / +25% / +25% over the first 14 days). The diagonal-striped band is the August dead zone — no new comms ship, but the in-app banner stays live with the "Coming 30 September" holding state.
Phase A scope. Phase A uses only push, email, and in-app banner (per v5 doc). No Promocard, Connect broadcast, LinkedIn, or app store presence in Phase A. Pulse 1 (~15 Jul) lands before HCPs check out for August; Pulse 2 (~3 Sep) is the post-holiday reintroduction with deadline foreshadowing.
Movement 3 note. Movement 3 messaging carries both consequence framing ("you'll lose access if you don't migrate") and recovery information ("here's what creating a new account will look like"). This seeds Phase C so the cutoff isn't a surprise.
Phase C scope. Phase C is CS-led and reactive — no scheduled push, email, broadcast, or Promocard. The active surfaces are the in-app "locked-out" message (visible to users who attempt to log in with old credentials) and the help-centre article / public FAQ (live throughout Phase B as a passive seed, promoted in M3, featured in C). Push/email/Promocard/broadcast lanes are intentionally empty in Phase C.
