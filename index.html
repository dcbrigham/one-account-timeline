How to read this chart
Each market row shows the campaign cadence per channel. Circles are discrete moments (a push send, an email send, a broadcast). Bars are continuous (in-app banner, Promocard, help centre) — segmented to show how the content pack changes between phases and movements. Phase B uses 4 push notifications + 3 emails per market — one push and one email in M1 (launch), two pushes and one email in M2 (benefit-led then deadline-led), one email and one push in M3 (escalation). Each wave narrows to non-converters. The diagonal-striped bands are the two dead zones — August (HCP summer holiday) and 21 Dec → 3 Jan (Christmas / New Year). No new comms ship in either window, but the in-app banner stays live with “Coming 26 October” (in August) and the M2 content pack (over Christmas). The red vertical line marks today.
Phase A scope. Phase A uses only push, email, and in-app banner. No Promocard, Connect broadcast, LinkedIn, or app store presence in Phase A. Pulse 1 (~15 Jul) lands before HCPs check out for August; Pulse 2 (24 Sep) confirms the 26 October launch and sets expectations — deliberately late enough to clear the busy early-September comms period, and close enough to launch that recall holds.
Movement 3 note. Movement 3 messaging carries both consequence framing (“you'll lose access if you don't migrate”) and recovery information (“here's what creating a new account will look like”). This seeds Phase C so the cutoff isn't a surprise.
Phase C scope. Phase C is CS-led and reactive — no scheduled push, email, broadcast, or Promocard. The active surfaces are the in-app “locked-out” message (visible to users who attempt to log in with old credentials) and the help-centre article / public FAQ (live throughout Phase B as a passive seed, promoted in M3, featured in C). Push/email/Promocard/broadcast lanes are intentionally empty in Phase C.
Cancelled campaigns. Campaigns marked as cancelled appear muted with a red strikethrough (markers) or hashed pattern (bars). The tooltip surfaces the cancellation reason. Toggle “Hide cancelled” in the filter bar to remove them from view entirely.
Placeholder dates. Pulse 1 (~15 Jul) and Pulse 2 (24 Sep) are working dates. Launch (26 Oct 2026) is the optimistic go-live; cutoff (31 Jan 2027) is the working end-of-Phase-B date. Phase C end (30 Apr 2027) covers the ~3-month post-cutoff recovery window — adjust if the wrap-up timeline differs.
